# Week 2 Worklog (11/05/2026 – 17/05/2026)

#### 1. Weekly Objectives
- Study AWS Academy Module 1 and Module 2.
- Understand AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM).
- Learn IAM Policies, IAM Roles, and permission management.

#### 2. Technical Activities Summary
This week focused on AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM), one of the core security services in AWS. I studied the concepts of authentication and authorization, learned how IAM Users, Groups, Roles, and Policies work together, and understood the principle of least privilege. In addition, I explored IAM Policy syntax, including Allow, Deny, Resource ARN, and Action elements. Practical exercises were performed to configure user permissions and assign IAM Roles for secure access to AWS services.
